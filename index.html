📝 Data Collection Methodology

Sources: California Highway Patrol (CHP) incident reports, California Roadkill Observation System (CROS) citizen science observations, and Caltrans official records.

Accuracy: CROS data has 13-meter spatial accuracy with >97% species identification rate. Data spans 2009-2024 with continuous updates.

Coverage: ~15,000 miles of California state highways monitored. Note: Actual collision numbers are likely higher due to unreported incidents.
